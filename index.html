What we help with

Six kinds of everyday help, from people who do it every day.

Bathing and incontinence care is our whole focus. That's why families call us when they need it done safely and done right.

Shower assistance

Steady hands in and out of the shower. Non-slip mat down, grab bars checked, and help with every step so a slip never gets the chance.

Bed baths

A full, gentle wash without leaving bed. We keep an eye out for redness or early sores while we're there.

Brief & undergarment changes

Discreet, unhurried and on your schedule, so skin stays healthy and nobody feels rushed.

Toileting assistance

Help with transfers and positioning, and a quiet heads-up to family if something seems off.

Linen changes

Clean sheets, pulled smooth, with soiled linens handled the sanitary way. Daily, weekly, or whenever you need.

Personal care

Hair, teeth, shaving, getting dressed. The small routines that make the day feel like yours again.

On the day

What a visit with KLM actually looks like.

  1. We use your own products.

    Your soap, your shampoo, your lotion. No surprise irritation from something your skin isn't used to.

  2. Safety goes in first.

    Non-slip mat down and grab bars checked before the water runs. Every transfer gets a hand.

  3. Every step said out loud.

    Caregivers explain what comes next and check in as they go. You stay in charge of your own care.

  4. A second set of eyes on skin.

    Redness, a rash, the start of a pressure sore. We notice early and let family or your provider know.

  5. Fresh sheets before we go.

    If linens are on your plan, the bed is clean and smooth when we leave.

Straight answers

The questions families ask us first.

Still wondering about something? Call 602-369-2535 and ask. Talking it through is free.

Does insurance cover it?

Usually not. Custodial care like bathing isn't covered by Medicare, Medicaid or most commercial plans. A private long-term care policy may reimburse or pay us directly, and if you already qualify for the VA's Aid & Attendance benefit, we'll look at that with you.

Can I book just one visit?

Yes. "Just a bath" is a real request we hear all the time. One visit, a few weeks, or a standing schedule all start the same way: a free consult.

Do you offer full-time caregiving?

No, and that's by design. We focus on bathing and incontinence visits so we can do them exceptionally well, on the days you need them.

What happens at the free consult?

We talk through the care you need, the days and times that work, anything we should know, and how you'd like to pay. Then we build your care plan and match you with a caregiver.

How do I pay?

Weekly or monthly, whichever suits you. Pay by check, or by credit or debit card through our secure online invoicing.

How are caregivers trained?

Every KLM caregiver is certified through a training and testing process before they work with clients, and keeps learning through our annual caregiver support program.
